# Catalogue methodology / 目录方法

This snapshot preserves the locally accepted public projection of the systematic search completed on 2026-10-05. It is a dated index, not a live eligibility check.

- Official programme discovery checked a frozen list of 62 platforms plus 3 extensions, tried at least two entrypoints and performed two gap follow-ups. Attempts are not counted as independent successful evidence. The coverage list retains no-confirmed, pending and ended/paid-only outcomes.
- The 73 official records retain primary source links, body SHA-256 references, observation times, billing and eligibility conditions. Operator labels distinguish model vendors, inference operators and platform programmes.
- The 34 third-party mechanism cards represent 24 announcement families across 19 sources; cards are not 34 guaranteed redeemable benefits. Announcements retain their units, conflicting/disabled statements, payment conditions and original dates.
- No account-specific eligibility, redemption, cash exchange, upstream authenticity or endpoint quality is inferred. Unknown expiry stays unknown. Reformatting does not extend an observation window.
- Private full-page captures and operational datasets are excluded. SHA references identify evidence; they do not grant redistribution rights or prove that a source remains unchanged today.
