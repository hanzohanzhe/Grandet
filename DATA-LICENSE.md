# Data licensing boundary

AGPL-3.0-only covers this hub's software and original project documentation;
it does not license provider datasets, vendor source material or trademarks.

The separately identified factual compilation is data/free-tokens.json and
its generated readable view CATALOGUE.md. See [data/MANIFEST.json](data/MANIFEST.json)
for each record's source owner, observation date, evidence references and
publication scope. Grandet's factual summaries are published as attributed
research; no blanket open-data license or sublicense of supplier statements
is asserted. Public web accessibility alone is not redistribution permission.
Vendor fullStatement and excerpt text, private captured page bodies, account
records and operational datasets are excluded. Source links and SHA references
do not grant rights to their target materials.

Future additions require a reviewed source/owner/date/rights manifest and
complete eligibility conditions. Do not infer data rights from the software
license. This is an independent catalogue hub, not a release of the full client
or its private catalogue.
